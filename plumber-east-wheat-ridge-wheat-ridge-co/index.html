The City of Wheat Ridge names East Wheat Ridge as one of its neighborhoods. It is the part of the city closest to Denver and Edgewater, which means the street address matters more than the ZIP code. The 80212 and 80214 ZIPs also reach into neighboring places.

Edgewater is a separate city with its own utilities and permit office, so this page covers only addresses inside Wheat Ridge.

What we can confirm about East Wheat Ridge

A Homes.com guide places the area near Sheridan Boulevard, W 38th Avenue and W 44th Avenue, with Interstate 70 to the north. It names Panorama Park, Founders Park and Hopper Hollow Park among the green spaces.

The guide reports older brick bungalows, some duplexes and newer larger builds, and a median build year of 1946. That is a neighborhood statistic, so we do not treat it as a fact about your house.

Local note

Check which city you are in

Because Edgewater and Denver sit next to this area, the first question on a call is the street address. Water, sewer and permits differ by city. If the address is in Wheat Ridge, the city's Building Division handles permits, and the permit desk is at (303) 235-2855, option 1.

Permit rules vary by job, so ask the Wheat Ridge Building Division what your repair needs.

General plumbing information, not specific to East Wheat Ridge

When an older drain line backs up more than once, a camera inspection shows whether the line needs cleaning or repair. When water supply pipes leak in several places, repiping may cost less over time than repeated patches. A plumber decides this by looking at the pipes.

Basements and crawlspaces show leaks first as damp walls or a musty smell. Shut off water to the area and call if you see a spreading wet spot.

What we do not know

We have no verified data on pipe materials or utility providers for individual streets in East Wheat Ridge. We do not claim any local plumbing condition. Your bills name your providers.
