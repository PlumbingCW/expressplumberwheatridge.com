What makes plumbing in Arvada different

Before we arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak in Arvada

Water spreads fast, and finished basements are common here. These four steps limit the damage while the plumber is on the way.

Shut off the main valve

Look where the water line enters the house, often a basement wall or a utility closet. Turn the valve clockwise until it stops. If the meter pit is outside, a city or district crew may need to close it.

Cut power to wet areas

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, leave that breaker alone and keep out of standing water. Switch off the breakers for the wet rooms only if you can reach them dry.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor. Open a drain or run a wet vacuum if you have one, and set buckets under steady drips.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher your street, the nearest cross street and where the water is coming from. A photo of the shutoff and the damage helps the plumber plan.

Local note

Who owns the pipe, the city or you

Most Arvada addresses are billed by the City of Arvada for both water and sewer. A few are not, and the special districts include Clear Creek Sanitation on Harlan Street and Ralston Valley Water and Sanitation. The city page does not list which blocks each serves, so look at your bill before you call a utility.

The utility handles the main in the street and you generally handle the lateral on your property, though where the line changes hands varies. Ask your provider where its responsibility ends.

Olde Town, older buildings and permits

Shops and apartments around Olde Town often share walls and stacks, so a leak can show up two units away. We trace before we cut.

Permit work in Arvada runs through the City of Arvada Building Division, whose plumbing inspectors review plans and inspect in the field. The application names a licensed plumbing contractor of record who is registered with the city, and inspectors can check licences on site.

Work on supply and drain lines follows a model code such as the UPC or IPC as adopted locally, and the Wheat Ridge Building Division is the source for the edition and any amendments.

Is emergency service available in Arvada?

Yes. The emergency line runs 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. When you call, give your street and the symptom, and the dispatcher sends a licensed plumber. Same-day appointments are offered for non-urgent jobs when the schedule has room.

Does the City of Arvada fix a leak inside my house?

No. The city or your district maintains its own mains, but the piping inside your walls and on your side of the meter is the property owner's responsibility. If the water is coming from the street, call the utility named on your bill.

Who pulls the permit for a water heater or sewer job?

When a contractor is hired, the contractor is responsible for obtaining the permit. In Arvada the application names a licensed contractor registered with the city, and the Building Division inspects the finished work.

Which office do I call for a sewer problem in Arvada?
