Fruitdale sits inside the City of Wheat Ridge, about 11 miles from downtown Denver according to the Homes.com neighborhood guide. That guide places Interstate 70 along its northern and western edges and describes W 44th Avenue as the road through the middle. Our shop is on W 38th Avenue, which touches the southern side.

This page covers plumbing calls from Fruitdale addresses. It lists what we can confirm about the area, what is general plumbing information, and what we do not know. The phone line is the only way to book, so there is no form to fill in.

What we can confirm about Fruitdale

Listing guides describe mostly ranch and ranch-style homes with some split-levels, wide lots, and a scattering of duplexes and condos. The same guide reports a median build year of 1979 for the area. That is a statistic for the neighborhood, not a statement about any one house.

The area includes Fruitdale Park, Prospect Park and Louise Turner Park, and the Clear Creek Trail passes nearby. If your property backs onto a park or open space, tell the dispatcher, because it can affect where a sump or drain discharge can go.

  • Fruitdale Park has an off-leash dog area and a disc golf course.
  • Prospect Park has a lake.
  • W 44th Avenue runs through the middle of the neighborhood.

Local note

Who handles sewer here

Many Fruitdale addresses fall inside the Fruitdale Sanitation District, which lists its service area as roughly Ward Road to Kipling Street and 44th to 52nd Avenue. Its office number is (303) 424-5476. Not every address in the neighborhood is inside that boundary, so your sewer bill is the reliable guide.

The district generally owns the main in the street. The service line from your house to that main is usually the owner's responsibility, but the district can confirm where the split is for your lot. Ask before any dig.

General plumbing information, not specific to Fruitdale

A slow floor drain, gurgling toilets and a wet basement corner can all trace back to a partly blocked sewer line. A camera inspection shows whether the cause is roots, a belly in the pipe or a broken joint. We do not assume the cause from the age of a home.

If wastewater is coming up through a floor drain, stop running water in the house and call. That is general safety advice and applies to any address.

What we do not know

We do not have verified data on pipe materials, pipe ages or common failures on specific Fruitdale streets. We will not guess. A plumber looks at your pipes and tells you what is actually there.
