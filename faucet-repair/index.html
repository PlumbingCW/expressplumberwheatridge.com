Emergency faucet and sink repair in Wheat Ridge covers anything at a fixture that leaks, sprays or will not turn off: kitchen and bath faucets, shower and tub valves, drain connections and the shutoff valves beneath the sink. The 24/7 line is open, and a licensed plumber can be sent right away. Until then, close the shutoff under the fixture or the main valve.

Fixtures fail in predictable places. Cartridges wear, O-rings dry out, stems corrode, and the little quarter-turn valves under a sink seize after years without being moved. We repair the part when it is worth saving and replace the fixture when it is not.

Do this first

When a fixture leak is an emergency

A supply valve that snaps or will not close, a pipe spraying under the vanity, or a shower valve soaking the wall behind it needs quick action. Water in a cabinet or wall cavity spreads before you can see it.

  1. Close the angle stop under the sink by turning it clockwise. If it will not move, go to the main.
  2. Find the main shutoff, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and close it.
  3. Open a lower faucet to drain the pipes and relieve pressure.
  4. Empty the cabinet and mop up standing water so the wood does not swell.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and describe where the water is coming from.

Broken or stuck shutoff valves under the sink

Angle stops and multi-turn valves under sinks are rarely used, so they seize or drip around the stem. Forcing one can snap the stem and turn a slow weep into a stream. If a valve will not close, do not keep cranking it.

We replace the failed valve with a quarter-turn type and new braided supply lines. If the valve is soldered to copper, the water to the house has to be shut off first, which is why a stubborn valve is often a main-shutoff job.

Symptoms

Where the leak is and what it means

Drip from the spout

A worn cartridge, washer or seat. Single-handle faucets usually need a new cartridge.

Water at the handle base

Dry O-rings or a loose packing nut.

Wet cabinet floor

A leaking supply line, a loose compression nut or a sink drain slip joint.

Low pressure at one tap

A clogged aerator or mineral-scaled cartridge, not a main supply problem.

Shower hot and cold fighting

A worn mixing cartridge or a pressure-balance valve that needs replacing.

Shower valves, bathtub faucets and tub drains

A shower valve that drips after you shut it off, or a showerhead that leaks from the arm, is usually a worn cartridge, a failed diverter or a loose threaded connection. Replacing a valve body behind tile means opening the wall, so we confirm access before quoting.

A bathtub faucet repair is similar to a sink repair, but the stems are often longer and the escutcheon hides the fix. Slow tub and shower drains are normally hair collecting at the strainer or the stopper linkage. Pull the cover and clear it first. If the water still stands, there is a clog deeper in the line and we can run a small cable through the overflow or drain.

Local note

Wheat Ridge water and fixture wear

Wheat Ridge is served by four water providers, and the city does not say which one reaches your street. Denver Water describes its southern system as moderately hard and its northern system as softer. White crust on faucet tips and sluggish handles are common signs of hardness.

Check your water bill for the provider, ask which system feeds your address and test your tap. If scale is the cause, soaking aerators in vinegar helps, and a clean cartridge swap resets the wear.

Installers in Colorado work to a model plumbing code, commonly the UPC or IPC family, with local amendments, so the permit desk is the place to confirm what applies at your address.

FAQs

Common questions

Do you handle faucet and sink repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheat Ridge?

Yes. The emergency line is open 24/7. Shut the valve under the fixture or at the main, then call and a licensed plumber is dispatched. Lower-urgency repairs may be booked same-day when available.

My shutoff valve under the sink will not turn. What should I do?

Do not force it. A seized stem can snap. Shut the main and call us to replace the valve with a quarter-turn type.

Why does my shower keep dripping after I turn it off?

A worn cartridge or a bad seat inside the shower valve. We pull the trim, check the stem and replace the part.

Can you fix a bathtub faucet without opening the wall?

Often yes. Most cartridges and stems come out through the handle. If the valve body itself is cracked, access from behind may be required.

Is a slow shower drain a plumbing emergency?

Not unless water rises or sewage smell appears. Clear the strainer first. If it is still slow, we can cable the drain.

Do you work for landlords and property managers?

Yes. Give us the tenant contact and unit number, and we handle the repair and explain what was found.
