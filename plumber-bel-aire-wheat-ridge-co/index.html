Bel Aire is one of the Wheat Ridge neighborhoods named by the city, and our shop on W 38th Avenue is close to its main road. A Homes.com guide places Crown Hill Park at the southern edge and Discovery Park to the west, with Kipling Street nearby.

This page lists what we can confirm about Bel Aire, some general plumbing information, and what we do not know.

What we can confirm about Bel Aire

The guide describes mostly brick ranch and split-level houses, with some townhomes and condos. It reports a median build year of 1956, which is a neighborhood statistic and not a claim about any single house.

It also lists Valencia Condominiums as a community for residents 55 and older. In a condo or townhome, the association usually owns the building's main lines and the shutoff for a stack, so check with the manager before you book a repair inside a shared wall.

General plumbing information, not specific to Bel Aire

A water heater tank that is leaking from the base is past repair and needs replacement. A tank with no hot water, a pilot light that will not stay lit or a tripping breaker is often repairable. The unit itself tells the story, not the neighborhood.

For a toilet that runs, a faucet that drips or a drain that is slow, a quick call can usually tell you if it is a small fix or a bigger one.

  • Find your main water shutoff before you need it.
  • Turn off the water to a leaking fixture at its own valve.
  • Take a photo of any label on the water heater before you call.

What we do not know

We do not have verified data on the pipe materials or water and sewer providers street by street in Bel Aire. Your bill names your providers. We also do not claim any special local plumbing condition for the area. A plumber looks at the problem and reports what is there.
