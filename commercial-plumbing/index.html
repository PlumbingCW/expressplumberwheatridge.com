Emergency commercial plumbing in Wheat Ridge covers leaks, drain backups and fixture failures at shops, offices, restaurants and multifamily buildings, at any hour. The 24/7 line is open, and a licensed plumber can be dispatched to your address. While you wait, close the building's main shutoff and move stock or paperwork off the floor.

Do this first

A building emergency after hours

A ceiling dripping into a sales floor, a floor drain pushing water up in a kitchen or a restroom overflowing into a corridor needs a quick decision. Water travels along beams and under flooring, so the visible spot is rarely the source.

  1. Close the building main or the isolation valve for the affected area.
  2. Shut off power to wet areas only if it is safe to reach the panel.
  3. Move inventory, files and equipment off the floor.
  4. Photograph the damage for your records.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and tell us the building type, the floor and who can give access.

Commercial leak detection

A hidden leak in a commercial building can run for weeks before the water bill shows it. We use acoustic listening gear, pressure testing and thermal imaging to find where water is escaping inside walls, slab and ceiling plenum space without tearing out finishes at random.

Tell us if the leak is on a domestic water line, a heating loop, a roof drain or a fire sprinkler branch. Sprinkler and fire systems need the right licensed contractor, and we say so if that is what the leak involves. The right answer may be a meter test first, then a section-by-section isolation.

Grease traps, storm drains and floor drain overflow

Restaurants and cafes depend on a grease interceptor to stop fats from reaching the sewer. When it fills, the kitchen drains slow, smell and eventually back up. Cleaning schedules vary by size and volume, and pumping is typically a licensed hauler's job, so we focus on opening the branch line and checking the baffles and inlet.

Floor drains and storm drains in parking lots, loading docks and mechanical rooms catch debris and sediment. A drain choked with grit overflows in a downpour. We clear them with a cable or a jetter and check the trap seal so sewer gas does not escape.

Backflow testing for businesses

Commercial properties with irrigation, fire lines, boilers or chemical processes usually carry backflow prevention devices. Many water providers require a certified test on a schedule, often yearly, and the paperwork has to reach the provider. The requirements vary by provider and hazard level.

We test and repair double check and reduced-pressure assemblies and replace failed parts. Tell us which provider bills your building, since four providers serve Wheat Ridge and each handles reporting its own way.

What to do before a crew reaches your building

Shut the main or the nearest isolation valve, move stock and records away from the water, and keep staff and customers clear of wet floors. Do not use a drain that is backing up, and do not enter a space where sewer odor is strong.

When to call 911 first: any gas smell, any water touching live electrical equipment, or a flooded space with people inside. Then call us for the plumbing side.

Work on supply and drain lines follows a model code such as the UPC or IPC as adopted locally, and the Wheat Ridge Building Division is the source for the edition and any amendments.
