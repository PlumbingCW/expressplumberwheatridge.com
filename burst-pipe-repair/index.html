Burst pipe repair means stopping an uncontrolled release of water, cutting out the damaged section and rejoining the line with sound pipe and fittings. In Wheat Ridge, the emergency line answers 24/7, so a 2 a.m. spray from a wall gets the same attention as a noon call.

Your first job is the shutoff. Find the main valve, usually near the water heater, where the line enters the house or in a basement or utility closet. Turn a round handle clockwise, or turn a lever a quarter turn so it sits across the pipe. Then open the lowest faucet to drain what is left.

Do this first

What to do when a pipe bursts

Water and electricity are a bad mix. If water is running near outlets, a panel or appliances, stay out of that room and do not touch the breaker with wet hands.

Once the water is off, the goal is to limit damage until a plumber arrives. Wet drywall and insulation can start to sag or grow mold within a day or two, so move fast on what you can control.

  1. Close the main shutoff valve and open the lowest faucet.
  2. Turn off the water heater so the elements or burner do not run dry.
  3. Mop or vacuum standing water and lift rugs and furniture off wet floors.
  4. Photograph every damaged area for your insurance claim.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and describe where the water is coming from.
Symptoms

Signs of a cracked or broken pipe

Sudden loss of pressure

Faucets sputter or run weak at every fixture, which often means a main or branch line has split.

A hissing wall

You hear spraying or rushing water behind drywall, even with nothing in use.

Wet, bubbling drywall

Paint blisters and a soft, dark patch spreads along a wall or ceiling.

Rust or brown water

Corroded galvanized lines may show discolored water before they finally let go.

Slow drain with a wet floor

A cracked drain pipe leaks wastewater around the break, and the smell tells you it is not clean water.

Frozen pipe thaw and repair

Water expands about 9 percent when it freezes, and that pressure can split a pipe before you see a drop. The crack often does not leak until the ice melts, so a frozen line that thaws on its own can flood a wall hours later.

If a faucet gives only a trickle in cold weather, shut off the main and open that tap. Warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer or heating pad, working from the faucet toward the frozen section. Never use a torch or open flame.

We thaw lines with controlled heat, then inspect the pipe for splits, especially at elbows and along exterior walls, before turning the water back on. Any cracked section is cut out and replaced, so the repair holds when the pressure returns.

Pipe materials

Repairing copper, PEX and galvanized pipe

Copper and PEX

  • Copper splits from freezing or pinholes and is repaired with a cut-out and new soldered or press fittings.
  • PEX tolerates some freezing but can be torn by a fitting failure or a sharp bend.
  • PEX repairs use crimp, clamp or expansion couplings.

Galvanized steel

  • Old galvanized corrodes from the inside, so one repair may sit beside weak pipe.
  • Threaded couplings are often hard to turn after years of rust.
  • Several leaks in the same run are a sign to consider replacing the line.

We tell you whether a patch is sensible or whether the pipe is near the end of its life. If it is, our repiping work replaces it with a longer-lasting material.

Drain pipe, copper and other pipe leaks

A cracked drain pipe is a different job from a pressurized line. Cast iron, ABS and PVC drains fail at joints, through corrosion or after something heavy settles on them. The fix is a cut-out and new pipe, and the area is cleaned afterward because the water is dirty.

A pinhole in copper often looks minor but can spray enough to stain a ceiling. Small leaks at a fitting are repaired the same way as bursts, with a new coupling or a short replacement section.

Local note

Who handles what in Wheat Ridge

Wheat Ridge is a limited-service city, so water is not provided by the city. A burst on the street side of your meter belongs to your water provider, while a burst inside your property is the homeowner's to fix. Your water bill names the provider, so keep its number handy.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Wheat Ridge Building Division. When a licensed plumbing contractor does the work, the contractor handles the permit, as state rules put that responsibility on the contractor.

Pipe sizing, venting and trap rules come from model plumbing codes like the UPC and IPC, which each city adopts in its own form, so permit rules vary and the Building Division has the answer.

FAQs

Common questions

Do you handle burst pipe repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheat Ridge?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls on nights, weekends and holidays. The dispatcher walks you through the shutoff while a licensed plumber is sent.

Should I turn off the water heater after a pipe bursts?

Yes, once the main is closed. A tank heater with no incoming water can overheat its elements, and turning it off removes that risk.

Can a frozen pipe be repaired, or does it need replacing?

Most splits are repaired by replacing the cracked section. If the pipe is corroded or has split in several places, replacing a longer run makes more sense.

Is a burst pipe covered by homeowners insurance?

Sudden water damage often is, though policies differ. Photograph everything, save damaged items and call your insurer early.

How do I know if the break is mine or my water provider's?

If the leak is on your side of the meter, it is yours. Check your bill for your provider and call if water is coming up from the street side.

I own rental property. Can you handle a burst in a tenant's unit?

Yes. We coordinate access and describe the repair so you have records for the tenant and your insurer.
