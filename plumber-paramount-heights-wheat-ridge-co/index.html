The City of Wheat Ridge lists a Rolling Hills and Paramount Park neighborhood on its revitalization map. Listing sites label a nearby set of homes on Hillside, Skyline, Morningside and Paramount Parkway as Paramount Heights, and those addresses carry the 80215 ZIP.

No official boundary for Paramount Heights is published, so this page is a guide to the area and not a map. Use your street address when you call.

What we can confirm about Paramount Heights

A neighborhood listing site reports single-family homes built between 1953 and 1992, with two to four bedrooms and sizes from about 1,500 to just under 6,000 square feet. Those are ranges across the area, not a statement about any home.

Larger homes often have more bathrooms, more fixtures and a bigger water heater. That raises the number of things that can fail, but it does not tell us what has failed in yours.

General plumbing information, not specific to Paramount Heights

Homes with several bathrooms often show uneven hot water when one tank serves everything. A tankless unit or a second tank can help, and the right choice depends on how the household uses water.

Low pressure at one fixture usually points to a clog in that fixture. Low pressure everywhere points to the main valve, the pressure regulator or the service line. A plumber can check each in turn.

Water treatment

We do not publish a hardness figure for this area, because we have not verified one. If you see white scale on fixtures, your water provider can give you a current hardness report, and a plumber can tell you whether a softener or filter is worth adding.

What we do not know

We have no verified data on pipe materials, water providers or sewer providers for individual streets here. Your bills name your providers. We also do not make claims about slopes, soil or drainage at any lot.
